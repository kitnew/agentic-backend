# Penzión Grand — factual knowledge base

## Property overview

Penzión Grand is located directly in the centre of Košice. It has operated year-round since 2010.

The property has 27 rooms and a maximum capacity of 65 guests.

Its name on Booking.com is Penzión Grand.

Reception staff speak Slovak and English.

The property is not barrier-free and has no lift. Subject to room availability, a ground-floor room may be suitable for a wheelchair user.

## Location and transport

The nearest bus stop is Vodná, about five minutes away on foot.

Košice railway station is about fifteen minutes away on foot.

Košice Airport is about ten kilometres away, and the drive normally takes around fifteen minutes.

The property does not provide airport or railway-station transfers.

The property has a private, gated, unguarded car park with 14 spaces.

Parking costs 7.50 EUR per day.

Parking spaces cannot be reserved and are used on a first-come, first-served basis.

Buses and motorhomes cannot park in the property car park.

Guests may park before check-in and return later when their room is ready.

## Rooms and occupancy

There are no dedicated single rooms. One guest may use a two-bed room for single occupancy.

Among the two-bed rooms, 16 have a double-bed arrangement and 3 are twin rooms with separate beds.

A preference for a double bed or separate beds may be requested, but it is not guaranteed.

Room prices are per room per night, not per person.

Declared standard prices apply year-round except on selected dates.

Extra beds are not available.

Baby cots are available free of charge.

Pets are allowed for 10 EUR per pet per night.

Every adult requires one normal bed.

A child sleeping with a parent does not consume a separate bed.

A child using a cot does not consume a normal room bed.

A child requiring a separate bed counts toward room capacity.

Rooms are located on the ground, first, and second floors.

Room views may face Kováčska Street, the courtyard, or the internal hall.

The entire property is non-smoking.

There are no dedicated allergy rooms.

## Room facilities and housekeeping

Every room has:

- a private bathroom with a toilet;
- either a bath or a shower;
- a television with Slovak, Czech, and international channels;
- free Wi-Fi;
- central heating.

Some rooms have a desk.

Rooms do not have:

- air conditioning;
- an in-room safe;
- a minibar;
- a refrigerator.

A hair dryer and an iron can be borrowed from reception.

Rooms are cleaned every second day.

Towels are changed every two days.

Bed linen is changed every four days.

Extra towels, pillows, or additional cleaning may be requested at reception.

A guest may decline room cleaning.

## Breakfast and food

Buffet breakfast is included in the room price for staying guests.

Breakfast is served daily from 07:00 to 10:00 in the ground-floor breakfast room.

Non-resident guests may have breakfast for 4 EUR per person.

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and similar dietary requirements may be arranged in advance, subject to supplier availability.

Room-service breakfast is not available.

Half board and full board are not available.

Skipping breakfast does not reduce the room price.

A takeaway breakfast package may be requested for an early departure.

The property has no restaurant and does not serve lunch or dinner.

## Check-in and check-out

Standard check-in is from 14:00 to 22:00.

Standard check-out is by 10:00.

Early check-in is free when the room is already available.

Late check-out until 14:00 costs 15 EUR.

After 14:00, another full night is charged.

Guests must show an identity card or passport at arrival.

No deposit is required.

A late arrival after 22:00 must be arranged in advance.

For an arranged late arrival, the room keys are placed in a safe at the main entrance. The guest receives the PIN by SMS at around 22:00.

When parking is required, the gate remote may be placed in the safe with the keys. The remote must later be returned to the box on the main door.

For departure before reception opens, guests may leave the keys and any parking remote in the box on the main door.

For an early departure, the stay must be paid at reception on the previous day.

New reservation requests are accepted only until 22:00 local time.

## Guest services

Reception provides:

- luggage storage;
- tourist maps;
- information materials;
- a first-aid kit.

The property does not provide:

- a printer;
- a scanner;
- a reception safe;
- concierge service;
- bicycle rental;
- electric-vehicle charging.

## Reservations, changes, and cancellations

Guests may contact reception by telephone or email to make a reservation.

Reservations can normally be made up to one year in advance.

Reception needs the requested room type, stay dates, and guest name.

For stays longer than seven nights, a 10 percent room-price discount is available.

Group bookings may receive an individually agreed discount when they include:

- more than 20 people; or
- at least 10 rooms for more than one night.

There are no regular:

- senior discounts;
- student discounts;
- last-minute discounts;
- early-booking discounts;
- loyalty discounts;
- wellness packages;
- romantic packages.

Direct reservations and flexible-rate Booking.com reservations may be cancelled free of charge before arrival.

A non-refundable Booking.com rate is charged in full.

Standard reservations have no cancellation fee or no-show fee.

A 50 percent advance payment applies only to group or long-term stays.

The advance payment must be paid by bank transfer at least one month before arrival.

Booking.com reservation changes and cancellations should be handled through Booking.com.

Direct reservations may be changed or cancelled by contacting reception.

## Payment and invoices

Payment is made at arrival.

The property accepts:

- cash in euros;
- Visa;
- Mastercard.

There is no card surcharge.

Payment may be divided between several people.

Bank transfer and company invoices can be arranged in advance.

Guests should contact reception by email before payment to arrange invoice details.

A recreation-contribution invoice must be requested before payment and must list every staying guest.

When a Booking.com reservation was paid in advance, reception may still be able to issue an invoice later.

## City tax

Košice city tax is 3.50 EUR per person per night.

The city tax is paid separately at arrival.

The following guests are exempt:

- guests under 18;
- guests over 70;
- holders of a severe-disability card.

## House rules

The entire property is non-smoking.

Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00.

Parties and excessive noise are not allowed.

Room visitors are allowed during reception hours.

An overnight visitor requires reception approval and payment for the additional guest.

Unaccompanied guests may stay from the age of 16.

Guests may consume their own food and alcohol in their room.

Common areas are monitored by cameras.

## Emergencies

The emergency exits are:

- the main entrance;
- the rear staff entrance.

In a fire or medical emergency, call 112 and inform reception.

## Recreation, events, and groups

The property does not have:

- a sauna;
- a swimming pool;
- a gym;
- a jacuzzi;
- massage services;
- a games room;
- a conference room;
- a meeting room.

A children's corner is available in the internal hall.

Corporate events, team-building events, catering, and celebrations for up to approximately 30 people may be arranged with reception.

Such events must finish by 22:00.

Graduation parties are not hosted.
