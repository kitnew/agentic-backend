# Penzión Grand — factual knowledge base

## Basic information

Penzión Grand is at Kováčska 65, 040 01 Košice, directly in the city centre. It has operated year-round since 2010. The property has 27 rooms and a maximum capacity of 65 guests.

Reception email: recepcia@penziongrand.eu  
Website: www.penziongrand.eu  
Reception telephone: +421 908 992 447  
Booking.com name: Penzión Grand

Reception staff speak Slovak and English. Reception is open daily from 07:00 to 22:00 and is not open 24 hours.

The property is not barrier-free and has no lift. Subject to room availability, a ground-floor room may be suitable for a wheelchair user.

## Location and transport

The nearest bus stop is Vodná, about five minutes away on foot. Košice railway station is about fifteen minutes away on foot. Košice Airport is about ten kilometres away and the drive takes around fifteen minutes. The property does not provide airport or railway-station transfers.

The property has a private, gated, unguarded car park with 14 spaces. Parking costs 7.50 EUR per day. Spaces cannot be reserved and are used on a first-come, first-served basis. Buses and motorhomes cannot park there. Guests may park before check-in and return later when their room is ready.

## Rooms and prices

There are no single rooms. One guest can use a two-bed room for 55 EUR per room per night.

There are 19 two-bed rooms in total: 16 rooms with a double-bed arrangement and 3 twin rooms with separate beds. The standard price is 65 EUR per room per night. A preference for a double bed or separate beds may be requested but is not guaranteed.

There are 2 three-bed rooms at 85 EUR per room per night.

There are 6 four-bed rooms at 99 EUR per room per night.

All room prices are per room per night, not per person. Prices apply year-round except on selected dates. Extra beds are not available. Baby cots are available free of charge.

Pets are allowed for 10 EUR per pet per night.

Rooms are on the ground, first, and second floors. Views may face Kováčska Street, the courtyard, or the internal hall. The whole property is non-smoking. There are no dedicated allergy rooms.

## Room facilities and housekeeping

Every room has a private bathroom with a toilet and either a bath or shower, a television with Slovak, Czech, and international channels, free Wi-Fi, and central heating. Some rooms have a desk.

Rooms do not have air conditioning, an in-room safe, a minibar, or a refrigerator. A hair dryer and iron can be borrowed from reception.

Rooms are cleaned every second day. Towels are changed every two days and bed linen every four days. Extra towels, pillows, or additional cleaning can be requested at reception. A guest may decline cleaning.

## Breakfast and food

Buffet breakfast is included in the room price for staying guests. It is served daily from 07:00 to 10:00 in the ground-floor breakfast room.

Non-resident guests may have breakfast for 4 EUR per person. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and similar requirements may be arranged in advance, subject to supplier availability.

Room-service breakfast, half board, and full board are not available. Skipping breakfast does not reduce the room price. A takeaway breakfast package may be requested for an early departure. The property has no restaurant and does not serve lunch or dinner.

## Check-in and check-out

Standard check-in is from 14:00 to 22:00. Standard check-out is by 10:00. Early check-in is free when the room is available.

Late check-out until 14:00 costs 15 EUR. After 14:00, another full night is charged. Guests must show an identity card or passport at arrival. No deposit is required.

A late arrival after 22:00 must be arranged in advance. Keys are placed in a safe at the main entrance and the guest receives the PIN by SMS around 22:00. If parking is required, the gate remote can be placed in the safe with the keys and must later be returned to the box on the main door.

For departure before reception opens, keys and any parking remote can be left in the box on the main door. The stay must be paid at reception the previous day.

Same-day reservations are accepted only until 22:00.

## Services

Luggage storage, tourist maps, information materials, and a first-aid kit are available at reception.

The property does not provide a printer, scanner, reception safe, concierge service, bicycle rental, or electric-vehicle charging.

## Reservations, changes, and cancellation policy

Guests may contact reception by telephone or email to make a reservation. Reservations can normally be made up to one year in advance. Reception needs the room type, stay dates, and guest name.

For stays longer than seven nights, a 10 percent room-price discount is available. Group bookings of more than 20 people or at least 10 rooms for more than one night may receive an agreed discount. There are no regular senior, student, last-minute, early-bird, loyalty, wellness, or romantic-package discounts.

Direct reservations and flexible-rate Booking.com reservations may be cancelled free of charge before arrival. A non-refundable Booking.com rate is charged in full. Standard reservations have no cancellation or no-show fee.

A 50 percent advance payment applies only to group or long-term stays and must be paid by bank transfer at least one month before arrival.

Booking.com changes and cancellations should be handled through Booking.com. Direct reservations may be changed or cancelled by contacting reception.

## Payment and invoices

Payment is made at arrival. Cash, Visa, and Mastercard are accepted in euros with no card surcharge. Payment may be divided between several people.

Bank transfer and company invoices can be arranged in advance. Guests should email reception before payment to arrange invoice details. A recreation-contribution invoice must be requested before payment and list every staying guest. If Booking.com was paid in advance, reception may be able to issue the invoice later.

## City tax

Košice city tax is 3.50 EUR per person per night and is paid separately at arrival. Guests under 18, guests over 70, and holders of a severe-disability card are exempt.

## House rules and emergencies

The whole property is non-smoking. Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00; parties and excessive noise are not allowed.

Room visitors are allowed during reception hours. An overnight visitor requires reception approval and payment for the additional guest. Unaccompanied guests may stay from age 16. Guests may consume their own food and alcohol in their room.

Common areas are monitored by cameras. Emergency exits are the main entrance and the rear staff entrance. In a fire or medical emergency, call 112 and inform reception.

## Recreation, groups, and nearby places

The property has no sauna, swimming pool, gym, jacuzzi, massage service, games room, conference room, or meeting room. A children's corner is available in the internal hall.

Corporate events, team-building events, catering, and celebrations of up to about 30 people may be arranged with reception and must finish by 22:00. Graduation parties are not hosted.

Nearby landmarks include St Elizabeth's Cathedral, the State Theatre, and the East Slovak Museum. Jahodná ski area is around twenty minutes away by car. Rumanová outdoor pool is about five minutes away on foot and the municipal indoor pool about ten minutes away.

Nearby restaurants include Central Pub, Pizza Napoli, Grange Burger, and Pizzeria Zvon. Urban shopping centre, with a supermarket and pharmacy, is about 250 metres away.

Guests may leave reviews on Google or Booking.com. The property has no social-media profiles. Compliments and complaints can be sent to reception in person, by telephone, or by email.
