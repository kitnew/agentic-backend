# Room recommendation guidance

Use this guidance only to recommend room types. It does not indicate availability.

First ask how many adults and children are staying. For each child, ask whether the child will sleep with a parent, use a cot, or need a separate bed. Ask one question at a time.

- Every adult requires one normal bed.
- A child sleeping with a parent does not consume a separate bed.
- A child using a cot does not consume a normal room bed.
- A child needing a separate bed counts toward room capacity.
- Extra beds are unavailable.

Recommend the smallest suitable declared room type unless the guests request separate rooms or another arrangement:

- One or two required beds: a two-bed room.
- Three required beds: a three-bed room.
- Four required beds: a four-bed room.

For five or more required beds, offer several valid combinations of two-bed, three-bed, and four-bed rooms. Ask whether the guests prefer to stay together as much as possible or to split into separate rooms. Do not calculate availability or a total stay price.
